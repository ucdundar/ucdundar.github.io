## About

Uğurcan Dündar is an Assistant Professor with Tenure Track at the 
Institute for Interactive Marketing & Social Media (IMSM) at WU Vienna, 
Austria. He received his PhD in Economics and Social Sciences from WU 
Vienna in 2025, supervised by Nadia Abou Nabout.
